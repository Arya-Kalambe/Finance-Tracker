# 💰 Finance Tracker — Personal Finance Management Dashboard

📊 **Finance Tracker** is a smart and interactive personal finance management web application designed to help users track their income, manage expenses, plan monthly budgets, and make informed financial decisions. It provides a centralized dashboard to monitor financial activity, analyze spending habits, and maintain better control over money.

## ✨ Key Features

- 💵 **Income Management:** Add and manage your monthly income to establish a clear financial overview.
- 📂 **Category-Wise Budgeting:** Allocate budgets to different categories such as food, shopping, transportation, education, entertainment, bills, and savings.
- 💸 **Expense Tracking:** Record daily expenses and monitor where your money goes.
- 📈 **Budget Analysis:** Compare allocated budgets with actual spending to identify spending patterns.
- 🟢 **Remaining Balance:** View the money remaining in each category and your overall available balance.
- 🔴 **Overspending Alerts:** Identify categories where expenses exceed the allocated budget through clear, color-coded indicators.
- 📊 **Interactive Dashboard:** Visualize income, expenses, savings, and spending distribution through charts, graphs, and summary cards.
- 🎯 **Savings Goals:** Set financial goals and track your progress toward achieving them.
- 📅 **Monthly Reports:** Review monthly financial performance, compare spending across months, and identify opportunities to save.
- 🧠 **Financial Insights:** Understand spending habits and discover areas where expenses can be reduced.
- 📱 **Responsive UI:** Access your financial dashboard through a clean and user-friendly interface across different screen sizes.

## 🎨 Dashboard Highlights

- Green indicators for savings, positive balances, and budgets within limits.
- Red indicators for overspending and negative balances.
- Visual representations of category-wise spending and budget utilization.
- Quick summaries of total income, total expenses, remaining money, and savings.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of Finance Tracker is to simplify personal money management by combining expense tracking, budget planning, and financial analytics in one platform. It encourages responsible spending, improves financial awareness, and helps users build better saving habits through data-driven insights.
